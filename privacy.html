Privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026. Fancy is a private assistant used by one person, its owner. This page explains what it does with Google user data.

1

Google Calendar data

  • What is accessed. Events in the owner's Google Calendar, through the Google Calendar API, after she signs in with Google and grants access.
  • How it is used. Only to answer the owner's own questions about her schedule in her Telegram chat, and to create or change events when she asks and confirms.
  • Sharing. Calendar data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models. To produce an answer, the relevant text is processed by the AI model provider that runs the assistant.
  • Storage. The access token is kept on a private server operated for the owner. Calendar data is not copied into any other system.
  • Removal. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, the stored token and chat notes are deleted.

Fancy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
